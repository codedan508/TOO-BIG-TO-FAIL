LIMITATIONS AND COVERAGE
- 167 valued records: funds, ETFs, CREF/TIAA accounts and 19 pension-related 13F portfolios. Unresolved plans are excluded rather than assigned zero assets.
- 137 asset fields have no explicit valuation date. Retrieval date does not imply current asset values. Other asset dates range from 2025-12-31 to 2026-08-31.
- Mutual funds use displayed fund assets. VFIAX and VTSAX explicitly use total parent-fund assets across share classes, confirmed with issuer. One representative ticker per fund. No extra standalone VOO or VTI entry added.
- Other third-party mutual-fund asset fields have not all been independently reconciled to issuer share-class totals.
- Pensions use only the reported 13F portfolio, not whole pension-plan assets. Total therefore mixes fund NAV and disclosed-securities value by design.
- Holdings are fixed to the two saved lists to isolate the weighting change. New asset dates can differ from saved holdings dates. This is not a refreshed current top-two portfolio.
- Newer CREF issuer factsheets show some different holdings, notably QCEQRX and QCSCRX. Those changes are not silently incorporated into the fixed-input weighting calculation.
- Stock names are aggregated at company level for Samsung shares and depositary receipts. Each parent gives at most one contribution to each company.
- Funds-of-funds, bond funds, and property accounts remain in the denominator even when their top two contain no direct stocks. No lookthrough stock votes are fabricated.
- FBIIX's flagged holdings contribute no stock score. Its available fund asset value is included in the denominator.
- Underlying holdings and pension ownership of included funds can overlap. The sum is gross reported assets, not deduplicated ultimate investor money.
- Washington's 13F covers only two securities; its weight must not be represented as Washington's full pension weight.
- Asset values are rounded to the precision supplied by sources. Calculated percentages are approximate.
- All stock scores use full fund weight per top-two stock as agreed. They measure voting influence; they are not actual dollars invested in those stocks.
